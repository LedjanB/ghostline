## Description

**It's 3:33 AM at St. Mercy Ward. Every light just went out. Something in the dark is hungry.**

Then a ghost named Boo grabs you by the collar and flies. Hold on. Whatever you do, don't let go.

Ghostline is a spooky (never gory) one-button runner. Hold to float, let go to fall, grab candles to keep your light alive, and get as deep as you can before the dark catches up.

**A one-way trip through 8 hand-painted worlds**
- St. Mercy Ward
- The Cold Room
- The Hollow Woods
- Crowsrest Cemetery
- The Dripping Dark
- The Bone Halls
- The Dead Carnival
- The Other Side

Each world has its own song, its own weather, its own giant lurking in the background, and a gate you fly through to get to the next one. No loops. The deeper you go, the stranger it gets.

**What's inside**
- 8 characters to unlock, each with a perk (Nurse Ivy, Dex the Hunter, Little Lila, Count Vlad, Fang...)
- 10 charms, equip 2 at a time
- 56 missions, levels and titles
- Spirit Rush, slow-mo, shields and candle magnets
- Dark chases: outrun a wall of nothing
- Grave Spin: spend souls for a second chance
- Daily Haunt: same run for everyone all day, race your own ghost
- Creative mode: you can't lose, play forever
- Free gift every 4 hours

**Plays everywhere**
Phone, tablet, PC, keyboard, mouse, touch or gamepad. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen for real fullscreen and offline play.

**Accessibility**
High contrast hazards, screen shake toggle, flash toggle, vibration toggle, separate music and sound volume.

**Your save stays with you**
Progress saves in your browser. Settings > Backup Save gives you a code you can paste on another device.
