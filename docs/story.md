# The Story of Vigil (short version)

Condensed from the Claude Doc "Ghostline: The Story of Vigil" (https://claude.ai/artifact/WG1rLy7keDUdu95qVYSxov).
Read this file, not the doc. Open the doc only for something this file doesn't cover.
Built so far: worlds 1 and 2 (allies + bosses), the shadow STRETCH, Boo only when needed.

## Core
- One night, one town (Vigil, on no map), 3:33 to sunrise. Milo (16, last patient at St. Mercy) is the only playable character.
- Under the hospital is a bottomless well. The dark comes up wherever no light burns. Tonight at 3:33 the power was cut, the generator didn't start, the boiler room door opened.
- Boo = Tobias Boone, night janitor 31 years, died on the stairs at 3:33 trying to start the generator. Still on shift.
- THE STRETCH = the dark wearing Boo's shadow (thrown long down the stairwell as his lantern rolled and went out). Never said out loud.
- Clues, planted, never explained: Boo has no shadow; its joints click like Boo's key ring; at the near stage it hums Boo's tune, a little off; Boo never looks at it; it never chases Boo; journal page 1 shows Boo with no shadow; in the ending a shadow slides back to Boo's feet.

## Who is on screen
- THE STRETCH: heard (steps, keys, breath, the hum) and seen as a long thin shadow from the left, longer the closer. Body only on failure: stumble = an arm lunges in; caught = the grab scene (the only time you see the grin). Tricks are shadow + sound tells.
- Boo: off screen. Milo carries Boo's lantern, which glows when Boo is near. Boo appears for flights, first-run saves, ally scenes, gates, the ending. In arenas: once per fight he carries Milo out of a big attack. Rare lines.

## Controls and powers
4 actions on every device, never more: move, jump, dodge, light. Attacks are automatic. Each power upgrades one action.
Phone: left thumb hold+slide = move, right tap = jump, right swipe = dodge, LIGHT button when charged.
Keyboard: A D move, W/Up/Space jump, S/Down/Shift dodge, E light. Gamepad: stick move, A jump, B dodge, X light.

Each chapter: run the world, meet the ally at the landmark (2 to 4 lines, no pause over 6 s), boss arena at the end, gate. The boss is built to need the new power.

## The 8 chapters
1. **Ward 4.** Rule: light pools (under a lamp it can't gain). Ivy: night nurse, 9 years, penlight and cold coffee. Boss **The Night Matron** (Sister Agathe): rolls on IV poles, lamps off, curtain sweeps (jump), stops to check each bed (hit her). Beaten: sits on the last bed, folds her hands. Landmark: the clock at 3:33, Boo's cap under it.
2. **The Cold Room.** Rule: frost floor (slides go twice as far, landings skid; when the hum stops a drawer moves). Ansel: unclaimed 40 years, sits up under his sheet, points, lies back. Boss **The Coroner** (Doctor Lisle): toe tags file you (slow 2 s), drawers as pushers and platforms, writes on his clipboard (hit him). Landmark: drawer 9, empty, sheet folded, still warm.
3. **Lamplight Park.** Rule: two paths, take the one with a lit lamp. Lila: lost in 1962, red raincoat, thinks she's late for dinner. Boss **The Park Keeper**: lamppost spine, broom arm; broom sweeps (jump), rake slams (dodge), lamp glass falls. Beaten: sees Lila, stops. Landmark: an oak grown around a lit lamp.
4. **Crowsrest.** Rule: noise wakes the crows and brings it closer. Dex: gravedigger's son, 17, wrong about ghosts. Boss **The Widow of Grave 400** (Agnes Wick): crow dives, grave bell shockwave (jump), hands from graves. Beaten: sits by the open grave, keeps waiting. Landmark: grave 400, open, a bell, fresh flowers.
5. **Old Vigil.** Rule: the tide rises every ~20 s, go high. Mott: rat-catcher 100 years dead, "young sir". Boss **Old Swallow**: pale thing with old Vigil's lamp glowing in its throat; lunges, pulls you to the edge, spits stones; hit the glow when it opens. Never speaks. Landmark: a door in the tunnel wall with a lit window.
6. **The Bone Halls.** Rule: echo, every trap comes twice (the copy hurts too). Nub: three-legged stray Boo fed for 6 years. Boss **The List-Keeper** (Brother Hesk): chalk lines (hop), staring skull beams (slide), falling letters; hit him when he steps back to read. Landmark: the Wall of Names, last name TOBIAS BOONE, under it in chalk M, I, L.
7. **The Midnight Fair.** Rule: the beat, everything moves on the music. Oskar: polite sad stilt man who walked the crowd home. Boss **Pell the Barker**: carousel horses on the beat, ticket blades, loudspeaker rings; hit him when he tips his hat. Landmark: the swan boat, sign ONE TICKET.
8. **The Bottom.** Rule: gravity flips at markers; all 7 rules return, faster. Tobias: Boo alive, mopping a looping hall, "Visiting hours are over, kid", gives a candy; Milo remembers. Boss **The Lamplighter**, then the ending as built.

## Villains
- THE STRETCH: never speaks. Hungry for light, doesn't hate Milo.
- The Lamplighter (Elias Wick, first keeper, went down the well): floor-length coat, wide hat, no face, a line of smoke, brass snuffer, ladder. Same height as THE STRETCH. From world 3 he snuffs a lamp and starts a Reality Shift. "SHH. IT'S LATE. LET IT BE DARK NOW."
- Pell the Barker (1931 fair): striped coat, dented top hat, grin too wide. Voice on loudspeakers from world 5. "STEP RIGHT UP, SON. ONE TICKET, ONE RIDE. EVERYBODY RIDES."
- Bosses 1 to 6 are not villains: people the dark got, still doing their old jobs.

## Reality Shift (from world 3, never in a boss fight or flight)
The Lamplighter snuffs a lamp: hush, colors drain, the world turns into its Other Side copy (100 years after the dark won, pale people doing what they did that night). Reach the next lit lamp (15 to 20 s). The world's rule turns against you, the shadow stays full length (one stumble and it's on you), shift-only candles and pages. World 8 is fully shifted.

## Voice
- One short line, all caps, simple words. Normal first, then one wrong thing: "400 GRAVES. ONE IS OPEN."
- Specific numbers: 3:33, 400, 1962, slab 9. Never "ancient", "cursed", "evil". Never explain. No jokes from the narrator; characters can be dry.
- Some moments get no text: scares, the Shift, the ending close-up. Never talk to the player about being a player.
- Boo: tired, kind, night shift coworker. "OKAY. OKAY. WE GO LEFT." "HOLD ON. I GOT YOU." "DON'T LOOK AT IT. I DON'T." "MY KNEES DON'T HURT. THAT'S NEW." "HEAR THAT? ... NEVER MIND." "I MOPPED THIS HALL EVERY NIGHT. IT WAS NEVER THIS LONG."
- Milo: quiet, dry, short. "OKAY." "NO. NO NO NO." "IS THAT THING SMILING?"
- Ally lines: Ivy "DON'T RUN ON MY WARD. RUN. RUN." · Ansel "LEFT DOOR. MIND THE STEP." · Lila "MY MOM SAID BE HOME WHEN THE LAMPS COME ON." · Dex "GHOSTS CAN'T CROSS RUNNING WATER. PROBABLY." · Mott "WATER'S COMING, YOUNG SIR. UP YOU GO." · Nub (sits, looks at the lantern, waits) · Oskar "I WALKED THEM ALL HOME. ALL BUT THE LAST NIGHT."
