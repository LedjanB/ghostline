Ghostline privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Ghostline does not collect, send or sell any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress (candles, unlocks, missions, settings, best distance and lifetime stats) is saved in your own browser's local storage on your device.
  • A small error log may also be kept in local storage on your device so the game can recover from crashes. It never leaves your device.
  • When you install Ghostline to your home screen, your browser caches the game files so it works offline.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Backup codes and sharing

Settings > Backup Save copies a code containing your progress to your clipboard. The Share button on the results screen opens your device's own share sheet with your distance. Both only happen when you tap them, and where that text goes is up to you.

Third parties

The game loads no third-party scripts, fonts or services. If you play on a hosting site (for example itch.io or GitHub Pages), that site's own privacy policy covers the page that hosts the game.

Children

Ghostline collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/LedjanB/ghostline.
